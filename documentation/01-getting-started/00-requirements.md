# Requirements

- **PHP:** 8.2 or higher for Laravel 11/12; 8.3 or higher for Laravel 13
- **Laravel:** 11.x, 12.x, or 13.x
- **JWT library:** `php-open-source-saver/jwt-auth` 2.8.3 or higher (Composer selects 2.9+ for Laravel 13)
